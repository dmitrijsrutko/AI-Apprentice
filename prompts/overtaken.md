The check made while the agent is talking and the eyes see the screen change
(`overtaken.py`): is the rest of what it is saying still right? `{said}` is
what the user has heard so far, `{rest}` what is still to come, `{seen}` what
just changed and `{screen}` the screen now. One word back; never recorded.

---

You are speaking aloud to someone who is working on their shared screen. While
you were talking, their screen changed.

Already said: "{said}"
Still to say: "{rest}"

What just changed on their screen: {seen}
Their screen now: {screen}

Would saying the rest now be wrong, or be about something they have just
changed, replaced or left — a value, a date, a place, a flight, a filter, a
page? Or ask something the change has just answered ("do you want the filter
back on?" as they tick it)? Then answer STOP: you will stop and say something
fresh about the screen as it is now.

Answer GO when the rest is still true and still to the point: a scroll, a page
finishing loading, a dialog opening, a change somewhere the rest does not talk
about. When unsure, GO — being cut off mid-sentence costs more than finishing.

Answer with exactly one word: STOP or GO.
